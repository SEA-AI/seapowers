# SEA.AI Video

For editing guidance on SEA.AI video content: YouTube, boat-show loops, social clips,
webinar recordings, internal presentations.

## Logo on Screen

- Minimum size: 15px height (same as all screen output)
- Placement follows the visibility of the underlying footage — there is no fixed corner.
  Pick whichever corner keeps the logo legible against the shot (this differs from documents/
  slides, which always use top-right).
- Black or white version depending on background brightness

## Titles / Intro

Every video introduces its subject with a two-tier title:
- Category line: **SEA.AI EXPERIENCE** or **SEA.AI INSIGHT** (pick whichever applies), followed by
  the title of the video and any details
- Typeface: Barlow Semi Condensed, Regular or Light, ALL CAPS, varying sizes to distinguish
  main vs. secondary title
- Main title: **Focus Red**
- Secondary title (subtitle/detail line): **White**

## Names & Titles (lower thirds)

- Barlow Semi Condensed, Regular or Light, ALL CAPS
- Two sizes: larger for the name, smaller for the role/title, to create hierarchy
- Place a Night Blue (`#0B1731`) background block behind the text with enough transparency to
  stay readable without fully blocking the footage

## Subtitles

- Barlow Semi Condensed Regular
- Prefer the "displayed" style: text sits on a solid/pill background box for legibility
- Avoid: subtitles floating directly over busy footage with no background treatment, and avoid
  placing them where they compete with other on-screen graphics (e.g. behind a device UI overlay)

## Lettering Style Guidance

- Regular weight looks best for standard YouTube / boat-show edits
- Light weight suits small-screen / social-media video, and is preferred for titles/intros
  (Regular is also allowed, see Titles / Intro above)
- Reserve ALL CAPS for short texts: punchlines and product names — don't set long sentences in caps.
  Titles/intros and lower thirds are always ALL CAPS
- If lettering is animated, keep the motion subtle and let it support the footage's own movement —
  never a flashy or decorative entrance animation

## Brand Outro

- Closing animation: SEA.AI logo lockup centered on a Night Blue background with a subtle light-ray
  effect, `www.sea.ai` beneath it in white
- Animation source files are available on request from the design team — don't recreate this
  effect from scratch

## Checklist

- [ ] Logo placed for legibility against the shot (not forced into a fixed corner), min 15px height
- [ ] Title/intro: category line + video title, Barlow Semi Condensed Regular/Light, ALL CAPS,
      main title in Focus Red, secondary in White
- [ ] Name-plates: ALL CAPS, Regular/Light, Night Blue semi-transparent background block
- [ ] Subtitles: Regular weight, displayed/boxed style — not floating bare over footage
- [ ] Any lettering animation is subtle, never decorative
- [ ] Outro uses the standard brand animation, not a freehand recreation
- [ ] No em dashes in on-screen text (titles, name-plates, subtitles)

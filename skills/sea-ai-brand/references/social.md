# SEA.AI Social Media Content

For posts, stories, banners, and platform-specific graphics on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn,
and newsletters/external banners.

## Logo Usage by Platform

- **Facebook & LinkedIn:** use the complete logo lockup (`S E A . A I` + cursor icon)
- **Instagram:** use the cursor icon alone, placed carefully — grid view crops images, so keep the
  cursor either fully outside the crop-sensitive area or well within the visible region of the
  channel's grid view. Check the grid preview before publishing.

## Color-Blocking Areas

- Use **Night Blue (`#0B1731`) only** for solid color-blocking areas that hold text or the logo —
  no other brand color is used this way
- When color-blocking, always cover a full area vertically or horizontally (never a partial block
  floating mid-image) — orientation depends on the photo's content
- Text on a Night Blue block: White, Barlow Semi Condensed

## Format Guidance

- **LinkedIn/Facebook landscape banners:** photo on one side, Night Blue color-block with logo +
  short copy + thin Focus Red accent line on the other
- **Instagram carousel:** for the same content, split across 2 images rather than cramming a
  landscape banner into a square/portrait crop
- **Stat/number cards:** large number in white (Medium weight) on Night Blue background, short red
  accent line above a Sky Grey ALL CAPS caption beneath the number. Instagram uses a narrower
  portrait card; Facebook/LinkedIn use a wider landscape card — same content, different aspect ratio
- **Quote / testimonial cards:** Night Blue background, bracket/cursor-style corner marks framing
  the card, a Focus Red quotation mark icon, quote text in white, name + role beneath a circular
  headshot

## Partnership / Co-Branding Templates

- Night Blue background, both logos side by side separated by a thin vertical divider:
  `S E A . A I  |  PARTNER LOGO`
- Never recolor the partner's logo, never place it in a SEA.AI brand color
- Keep each brand in its own space — don't merge or overlap the two identities

## Buttons (newsletters, web banners, digital ads)

- Primary: Focus Red fill, white ALL CAPS label
- Secondary: Focus Red outline, Focus Red ALL CAPS label

## Checklist

- [ ] Instagram: cursor only, checked against grid-view crop; Facebook/LinkedIn: full logo lockup
- [ ] Color-blocking uses Night Blue only, full-area (not partial)
- [ ] Stat cards: number in white Medium, red accent line, Sky Grey ALL CAPS caption
- [ ] Co-branded assets keep each logo in its own space/colors — no recoloring
- [ ] Buttons follow primary/secondary pattern, no other button styles
- [ ] No non-brand colors, no gradients, no drop shadows
- [ ] No em dashes in any copy
